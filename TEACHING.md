# Teaching

I taught in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Central Florida from 2006 to
2020, apart from the academic year 2012–2013, which I spent at MIT.

The courses I came back to most often were the two quantum ones, *Quantum Computing* and *Quantum
Information Theory*, and later *Machine Learning* and *Artificial Intelligence*. Alongside those I
taught the department's standard courses: algorithms and complexity, discrete structures,
programming languages, systems software, cryptography, compilers, and introductory programming.

The materials for the later courses are still on GitHub:

- [quantum_computing_slides](https://github.com/schneider128k/quantum_computing_slides) — Quantum Computing
- [quantum_info](https://github.com/schneider128k/quantum_info) — Quantum Information Theory
- [machine_learning_course](https://github.com/schneider128k/machine_learning_course) — Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence
- [ai_course](https://github.com/schneider128k/ai_course) — Advanced Artificial Intelligence
- [intro_compiler_course](https://github.com/schneider128k/intro_compiler_course) and [intro_haskell_programming](https://github.com/schneider128k/intro_haskell_programming)

The teaching from the Karlsruhe years is not here. What survives from that time is the lecture
notes and seminar work in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md), which I wrote as a student rather than gave as a
course.
