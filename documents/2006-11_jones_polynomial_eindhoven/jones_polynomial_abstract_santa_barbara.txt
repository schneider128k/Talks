Title: Quantum algorithms, quantum complexity theory and their
connection to the Jones polynomial (joint work with Jon Yard)

Abstract: I will present a proof that approximating the Jones
polynomial of plat closures of braids at roots of unity to within a
certain precision is BQP-complete, meaning that it is the hardest
problem that can be solved efficiently on a quantum computer.  This
result was essentially shown by Freedman, Kitaev, Larsen, and Wang in
the context of topological quantum computation. However, our proof,
which is motivated by [Aharonov, Jones, Landau 05], is more direct, as
it makes use of a conceptually simpler encoding of qubits into
irreducible representations of the braid group, while remaining within
the standard quantum computation model.  I will conclude with an
application to the complexity class Quantum-NP.
